# PRD — Anubhav Budek Portfolio

## 1. Summary
A single-page personal portfolio site for Anubhav Budek, a 2nd-year CSE undergraduate at GIET University, Gunupur. The site introduces him, shows his skills and one real project, and gives visitors a way to reach him.

## 2. Audience
Primary: **Recruiters and HR reviewers** screening for internships/entry-level roles. They skim in seconds, look for proof of ability first, and expect a fast path to contact info / a resume.

Secondary: General visitors (college network, LinkedIn, GitHub referrals).

## 3. Purpose
Primary stated purpose: **personal brand / content presence** — establish who Anubhav is, not just what he's credentialed in.
Note: this sits in some tension with the recruiter audience (see Rules.md → "Known tension"). Future content decisions should lean toward proof-of-ability first, personality second, without losing the personal voice.

## 4. Goals
- Make a strong first impression within the first screen (hero).
- Get a skimming recruiter to the proof (skills + project) fast.
- Present only real, verifiable information — no invented projects, certificates, or metrics.
- Give one unambiguous way to make contact.

## 5. Non-goals (for now)
- No blog / content-creation showcase yet (interest is mentioned, not demonstrated with artifacts).
- No backend, CMS, or contact form submission — contact is via `mailto:` and GitHub link only.
- No multi-page routing — single scrolling page with anchor navigation.

## 6. Content sections (current)
1. **Hero** — name, one-line identity, CTA to Projects and Contact.
2. **About** — background (GIET University, CSE, 2nd year/3rd sem), interests (football, content creation), stat highlights (CGPA, semester, matriculation score).
3. **Skills** — HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
4. **Projects** — Offline Internship Management App (built during internship at Munition India Limited).
5. **Education** — timeline: GIET University (ongoing) → Intermediate, IDM Public School (62.33%) → Matriculation, IDM Public School (91%).
6. **Achievements** — Munition India Limited internship; 91% matriculation score.
7. **Contact** — email (`anubhavbudek10@gmail.com`), GitHub (`github.com/TheSocialEngineer-01`).

## 7. Known gaps / expected next real content
- No resume/CV file linked yet — add a download link once available.
- Only one project — add more as they're built; don't pad with placeholders.
- No formal certifications yet — Achievements section stays lean until there are real ones to add.
- Consider an Experience section distinct from Projects once there's more than one internship/work item.

## 8. Success criteria
- A recruiter can identify: who this is, what he can do, and how to contact him, without scrolling past the fold more than twice.
- Every claim on the page is traceable to something the user actually stated — no fabricated proof points.
- Page works and looks correct on mobile (< 480px) and desktop, keyboard-navigable, respects reduced-motion.
